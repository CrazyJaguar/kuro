What We Believe
Four Principles
/ 01
Radical Transparency

Every coffee on our menu shows the farm name, elevation, harvest date, and price paid. Nothing is hidden.

/ 02
Zero Compromise

We'd rather close early and run out than serve a cup we're not proud of. Quality is not negotiable.

/ 03
Planet Account

Compostable everything. Electric delivery. 1% revenue to Himalayan reforestation. This is non-optional for us.

/ 04
Community Platform

KURO hosts 4+ events monthly. Local artists, musicians, farmers, thinkers. The café is just the stage.
